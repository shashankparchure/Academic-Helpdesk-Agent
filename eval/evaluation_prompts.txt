IIT Jodhpur Academic Helpdesk - Evaluation Prompts
==================================================

Total: 28 prompts (20 academic, 8 out of scope)
Division: 4 people, 7 prompts each

Evaluation procedure:
- Ask each prompt to both the single-agent and multi-agent versions.
- Use the same prompt wording and source/index setup for both versions.
- Record the answer, citations, whether an out-of-scope refusal was correct, and any unsupported claims.
- Some academic prompts intentionally test ambiguity or missing evidence. A careful answer may explain
  the limitation or ask for clarification instead of guessing.
